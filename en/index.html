Frequently asked questions

How is confidentiality handled?

With roles, permissions, logging and encryption. You decide who sees what.

Do we have to change our systems?

Rarely. What is missing is usually built around your current programs and connected to them.

Do you use AI on client data?

Only if you want to, and then preferably with a model on your own server so the data stays with you.

What does it cost?

It depends on the scope. After the first call you get a written proposal with a price.
