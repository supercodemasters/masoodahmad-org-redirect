Industries

IT and software for accounting firms

Less chasing records, more time for advice.

Accounting firms spend a lot of time chasing receipts and records, reminding about deadlines and moving data between systems. A client portal and automatic reminders reduce the chasing, and integrations reduce the typing.

Common challenges

  • Records arrive late and in the wrong format
  • Deadlines are tracked in spreadsheets
  • The same data is typed into several systems

What can be built

  • A client portal for uploading records
  • Automatic reminders before deadlines
  • Integrations between clients’ systems and the firm’s
  • A dashboard of status per client and period

Relevant experience

  • Finance and ERP system with chart of accounts and double-entry (Samsara Solutions, Bulgaria)
  • ACCA Certified Accounting Technician (levels A and B)

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace our accounting software?

No. It adds what sits around it: portal, reminders and connections.

Do you understand accounting?

Yes. I am an ACCA Certified Accounting Technician and have built finance modules with double-entry bookkeeping.
