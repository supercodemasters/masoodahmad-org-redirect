Industries

IT and software for marketing and advertising agencies

Client reports, campaigns and time without manual compilation.

Agencies spend a lot of time compiling results from ad tools and web statistics into client reports. With data in one place, reports can be created automatically and time spent on ideas.

Common challenges

  • Client reports built by hand every month
  • Time per client and campaign is hard to track
  • Data from many tools

What can be built

  • Automatic client reports from ad and web data
  • Time reporting per client and campaign
  • A client portal with material and approvals
  • A dashboard for profitability per client

Relevant experience

  • Google Analytics and Mixpanel dashboards (clients in the USA)
  • Automated email campaigns in a lead generation platform (own project 2026)

Frequently asked questions

Which tools can data be fetched from?

Most ad and analytics tools have APIs. We check your tools first.

Can clients approve material in the portal?

Yes, with comments and history.
