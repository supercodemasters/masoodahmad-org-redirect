Industries

IT and software for staffing and recruitment firms

Candidates, assignments and matching without searching the inbox.

Staffing and recruitment firms handle many candidates, CVs, assignments and schedules. CV parsing and AI matching can save a lot of time, with the recruiter still deciding.

Common challenges

  • CVs arrive in many formats
  • Candidates are searched by hand for each assignment
  • Schedules and time for placed staff tracked by hand

What can be built

  • A candidate database with CV parsing
  • Matching candidates to assignments
  • Schedules and timesheets for consultants
  • A client and candidate portal

Relevant experience

  • Lead generation and recruitment platform with 100,000+ candidates and CV parsing (own project 2026)

Frequently asked questions

How is candidate data handled under GDPR?

Consent, retention time and deletion are built into the system.

Does the AI decide about candidates?

No. AI sorts and suggests; the recruiter decides.
