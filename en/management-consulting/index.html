IT and software for consulting firms

Assignments, time and profitability without a monthly Excel puzzle.

Consulting firms sell time and knowledge. When assignments, timesheets, invoices and CVs live in different tools, it is hard to see utilisation and profitability, and to answer requests quickly.

Common challenges

  • Timesheets and invoices reconciled by hand
  • Hard to see utilisation ahead
  • CVs and references rarely updated

What can be built

  • Assignments and time reporting linked to invoicing
  • Utilisation and forecast per consultant
  • A CV bank that stays up to date
  • A dashboard for profitability per client and assignment

Frequently asked questions

Does it suit a small consultancy?

Yes. It can start with time and invoicing and grow from there.

Can CVs be generated automatically?

Yes. CVs in different formats can be created from the same data.
