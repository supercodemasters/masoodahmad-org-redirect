Industries

IT and software for administrative service firms

More capacity without more hands.

Firms that sell administration, customer service, translation or secretarial services live on efficiency. Automatic flows and AI for documents and email give more capacity without more staff.

Common challenges

  • Assignments arrive by email without structure
  • Documents are handled manually
  • Hard to see time per client

What can be built

  • A case flow from email and forms
  • AI that sorts and summarises incoming work
  • Document management with templates
  • Time per client linked to invoicing

Frequently asked questions

Can AI help a small firm?

Yes, for example sorting email and drafting replies, with a person approving.

How are client documents protected?

With permissions per client and logging of all access.
