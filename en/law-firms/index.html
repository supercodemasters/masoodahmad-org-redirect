Industries

IT and software for law firms

Cases, documents and time in one place, with the right permissions.

Law firms handle sensitive cases, many documents and billable time. Case management with permissions, document templates and time reporting reduces both risk and admin.

Common challenges

  • Documents saved in folders and email
  • Time reported late
  • Clients ask about status

What can be built

  • Case management with roles and permissions
  • Document templates and PDF generation
  • Time reporting per case linked to invoicing
  • A client portal with documents and status

Relevant experience

  • Case management system for a law firm with role-based access and PDF generation (CFN Teknoloji, Turkey)

Frequently asked questions

How is client data protected?

With role-based access, logging of every access and encryption.

Can documents be created from templates?

Yes. Case details are filled in automatically and the document saved as PDF.
